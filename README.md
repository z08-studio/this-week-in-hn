# This Week in HN

![This Week in HN — a weekly newsletter about stories, ideas, projects, and discussions from Hacker News](assets/banner.png)

**This Week in HN** is a weekly newsletter published every Friday, bringing together stories, projects, and discussions from [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/).

It covers technology, science, startups, culture, and inventive projects, with a curated selection of ideas and discoveries from the past week.

[Subscribe on Substack](https://thisweekinhn.substack.com) to receive the latest issue in your inbox.

Part of **This Week In Newsletters**, maintained by **z08 Studio**.
